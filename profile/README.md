# BaakoFlo

BaakoFlo is a technology and infrastructure company building the digital backbone
of West Africa. We design, deploy, and operate the systems that institutions and
businesses depend on - from fiber optic networks and data centers to enterprise
software and managed IT services.

## What We Do

- **Telecom Infrastructure** - Fiber optic network design, deployment, and ISP services
- **Data Centers** - Colocation and managed hosting for enterprises and institutions
- **Enterprise Software** - Hospital management systems, operational platforms, and
  custom business applications
- **Managed IT Services** - End-to-end networking, infrastructure, and support for
  businesses of all sizes

## Who We Serve

We work with hospitals, universities, commercial enterprises, and public institutions
across Ghana and the wider African region.

## Our Mission

To close the digital divide in Africa by delivering world-class technology
infrastructure and software solutions that are affordable, reliable, and built for
the communities we serve.
